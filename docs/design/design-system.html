Design system & components

The reusable foundation for every page — Premium Editorial × Professional Export Company. Concept A's calm, serif-led language carrying Concept C's trust devices (stat band, certification row, spec tables, RFQ). Less, but better: premium comes from spacing, type, proportion and consistency — not decoration. Faces approximated (Fraunces + Inter in production); images are labelled placeholders.

Responsive & motion

Breakpoints (mobile-first)
TokenWidthShift
base< 6401-col; hamburger nav; stacked CTAs; stat band 2×2; sticky bottom "Request a Quote" bar
sm/md640–10242-col grids; inline nav appears; spec tables scroll-x
lg> 1024Editorial split heroes; 4-col stat band; full timeline row; max container 1200–1280
Motion — allowed vs avoid
✓ Allowed: fade, reveal-on-scroll, gentle slide, soft hover elevation, image cross-fade. 150–300 ms, ease-out.
✗ Avoid: heavy parallax, decorative shapes, long/looping transitions, motion for its own sake.
All motion respects prefers-reduced-motion; performance always wins.
